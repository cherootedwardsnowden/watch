How it stays secure

After you click continue, we generate a private payment page tied to your IP address. Only you, on this device and network, can open it. The URL contains a long random slug, so it is not guessable, listable, or indexable.

When you choose a coin and network, the price for $5 is computed from the live exchange rate and locked in. The crypto address you see is generated for you alone, on demand, by NowPayments. Send the exact amount within five minutes. We poll the blockchain every ten seconds and confirm as soon as the transaction is broadcast.

Once your payment confirms, the system creates a fresh username and a long random password, hashes that password with bcrypt before saving, and emails the plaintext credentials to you over Maileroo SMTP with TLS. We never see, store, or log the password in plaintext on the server.

Logins are rate-limited per IP. Sessions are signed JWTs delivered through HttpOnly, SameSite cookies, never exposed to JavaScript. Inside the app, every video URL you receive is unique to you, your IP, and a five-minute window. Reload the page and you get a brand new URL. Share it with anyone else and it dies the moment another IP touches it.

Video segments are AES-128 encrypted and the decryption key is itself fetched through a token bound to your session. There is no plain MP4 to download, no progressive stream, no obvious file path - only short, signed, IP-locked HLS chunks. Right-click is disabled in the player and the source attribute is replaced after every load.

We use no analytics. We do not ask for your name, your card, or your phone. We only need an email to deliver credentials. After your subscription expires, your account remains, but premium streaming returns to a 15-second preview until you renew.
